Delete your KROWL account

For the KROWL apps for Windows and Android · Developer: GBro Dev · Updated November 11, 2026

You can ask for your KROWL account and the data that goes with it to be deleted at any time. It's free, and you don't have to give a reason.

How to ask

  1. Send an email to GBroDev1@gmail.com with the subject Delete my KROWL account.
  2. Send it from the email address on your KROWL account, so we know the request is really from you.
  3. Include your KROWL login name (the username you log in with).
  4. If you own an organization on KROWL, tell us whether to delete it or which member should become its new owner. An account that still owns an organization can't be deleted.

We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to let you know it's done. If the email doesn't come from the address on the account, we'll ask you to send it from that address first.

What is deleted

  • Your account: login name, email address, password, display name, profile picture, name color, and status.
  • Your sign-ins on every device.
  • Your friends list and friend requests.
  • Your direct message conversations, including the pictures and files in them. They're removed for the other person too.
  • Your list of blocked people.
  • Invites to organizations.
  • Your membership in every organization: your roles, levels, read marks, fleet, and reputation.

What is kept

  • Messages you posted in an organization's text channels stay there, so the organization's conversations still make sense. If you want a specific message removed, delete it in the app before you ask, or tell us in your email.
  • Connection history (when an account connected to an organization and for how long, with no messages) is kept for up to 7 days and then deleted.
  • Usage totals (how much time an account had KROWL open each day, with no messages or anything said in voice) are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted.
  • The service's security log, which records logins and the internet addresses they came from, is kept for a limited time and removed automatically as the log fills.
  • A backup copy of the deleted account is kept for up to 30 days in case the deletion was a mistake, and then deleted. Routine backups of the whole service made before the deletion are deleted on their normal schedule, within the same 30 days.
  • Reports about breaking the rules that involve the account may be kept, so that a banned person can't simply come back.

Deleting only some of your data

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove things. In the app you can delete your own messages and whole direct message conversations, change or remove your profile picture, and leave an organization. For anything else, email us at the address above.
