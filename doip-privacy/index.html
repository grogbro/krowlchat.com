Privacy Policy

Dwarves of Iron Peak companion app (Android, package com.rocketslug.doip)
Developer: GBro Dev (Greg Breault) · Effective date: November 10, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Dwarves of Iron Peak companion app ("the app") handles information. The app is a free companion to the Dwarves of Iron Peak tabletop board game. It provides a dice roller, quest tracker, character stats, the rule book, game music, and game news.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

Information stored on your device

Anything you do in the app, such as dice rolls, quest progress, or character choices, stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet use

The app uses an internet connection only to:

  • download the latest Dwarves of Iron Peak news posts, and
  • open web links you tap (for example, a link to buy the board game), which open in your browser or another app such as Amazon.

As with any web request, the server that delivers the news may receive standard technical information such as your IP address. We do not use this information to identify you. Websites and apps you open from a link have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load news and open links as described above.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer using the email address on the app's Google Play store page.
