KROWL Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Applies to the KROWL apps for Windows and Android (package com.gbrodev.krowl) and the KROWL service · Developer: GBro Dev

KROWL is a free app for communities and teams to chat and stay organized. This page explains what information KROWL collects, why, who can see it, how long it's kept, and how to have it deleted. The short version: KROWL collects what it needs to work as a chat app, has no ads and no tracking, and doesn't sell your data.

What we collect

Your account. When you create an account you give a login name, an email address, and a password. Your password is stored only as a salted hash, not as the password itself. You can also set a display name, a profile picture, a name color, and a status.

What you send and share. KROWL stores what you put into it so it can show it to the people you send it to:

  • Direct messages, including pictures and files you send in them.
  • Messages, pictures, and other content you post in an organization, and things you add to it such as fleet entries.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, and the people you've blocked.
  • Which organizations you belong to, and your roles and levels in them.

Voice and screen sharing are carried live through the KROWL service to the people in the same channel. KROWL doesn't record them.

Information the service records as you use it:

  • The internet (IP) address you sign up and log in from, in the service's security log.
  • When you connected to an organization and for how long.
  • How much time you had KROWL open each day, and which version of KROWL you use. This never includes what you wrote or said.

Reports. If you report someone, or someone reports you, the report is stored: who reported whom, the reason, and anything the reporter typed.

Email you send us, for example a request to delete your account.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising, and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking tools from other companies.
  • No location, contacts, phone number, or files from your device other than the ones you choose to send.
  • No payment information. Donations are handled by PayPal on PayPal's own pages.

The Android app

The Android app keeps a few things on your phone: a sign-in token (in Android's protected storage) so you stay logged in, your settings, and copies of profile pictures so they don't have to be downloaded again. Uninstalling the app removes them. It asks for one permission you can refuse, notifications, used only to alert you to new messages. It uses the internet only to talk to the KROWL service and to open the web pages linked in its settings.

Why we collect it

  • To run KROWL: logging you in, delivering your messages, showing your friends and organizations.
  • To keep KROWL safe: stopping abuse, acting on reports, and banning people who break the Terms of Use.
  • To keep KROWL working: seeing how much it's used and finding problems.
  • To answer you when you email us.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't build profiles of you.

Who can see it

  • Other people on KROWL see what you send them or post where they can read it, plus your display name, profile picture, and whether you're online (unless you set yourself to Invisible).
  • People who run an organization can see and manage what's posted in their organization and who its members are.
  • The people who run KROWL can access what's stored on the service when that's needed to keep it running, look into a report, or handle a request from you. Messages are encrypted on their way between your device and the service, and are stored on the service; they are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • KLIPY. When you search for a GIF or sticker, the words you search for are passed to KLIPY (klipy.com), which provides them. Your account details aren't sent with the search.
  • Our hosting providers. The KROWL service runs on a rented server, and this website is hosted by GitHub. They carry the data but don't use it.
  • The authorities, only when the law requires it or to report illegal content such as child sexual abuse material.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising.

How it's protected

Everything between the KROWL apps and the KROWL service travels over an encrypted connection. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Access to the server is limited to the people who run KROWL. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send anything through KROWL that would cause serious harm if it got out.

How long it's kept

  • Your account and what you've sent are kept until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Connection history is kept for up to 7 days.
  • Usage totals (time KROWL was open each day) are kept for up to 90 days.
  • The security log is kept for a limited time and removed automatically as the log fills.
  • Backups of the service are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Reports may be kept for as long as they're needed to keep banned people from coming back.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted at any time. Delete your KROWL account explains how, exactly what's deleted, and what's kept. You can also delete your own messages and direct message conversations in the app.

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to correct it, by emailing the address below from the email address on your account.

Children

KROWL is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
